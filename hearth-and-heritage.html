The Oakhaven Duology

The book

Hearth & Heritage begins with the things families leave behind: houses, names, expectations, loyalties, wounds, and stories everyone remembers differently.

At the centre of Oakhaven is the complicated question of what it means to belong somewhere — and whether belonging is something inherited or something a person gets to choose.

It is a story about family and intimacy, about old loyalties meeting new lives, and about the quiet courage required to decide which parts of the past deserve to survive.
