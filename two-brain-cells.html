Contemporary Romance

The book

Two Brain Cells is a contemporary romance about intelligent people, complicated feelings, and the spectacularly irrational things humans do when love enters the room.

It is a story about the spaces between certainty and doubt, competence and vulnerability, friendship and something that refuses to remain friendship.

Beneath the jokes, the arguments, the work, and the wonderfully inconvenient attraction is a simpler question:

What happens when two people who understand almost everything finally encounter something they cannot reason their way out of?
