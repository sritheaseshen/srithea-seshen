About

A writer between worlds.

Srithea Seshen

Srithea Seshen is a writer and scientist interested in intimacy, memory, mythology, ordinary life, and the complicated spaces between them.

Fiction comes first for her as a study of people: the choices they make, the stories they tell themselves, the ways they love, and the things they carry long after they believe they have left them behind.

Her work moves between contemporary romance, literary fiction, and the quieter territories of family, inheritance, desire, identity, and choice.

The writer

Fiction about people before plots.

The stories are interested in what happens underneath the obvious story: why people stay, why they leave, what makes forgiveness possible, and what it means to begin again.

The scientist

Curiosity is not confined to one discipline.

Alongside writing, Srithea works in science. That life shapes the way she observes the world: with curiosity, patience, skepticism, and a fascination with the hidden structures beneath visible things.
