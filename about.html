About

Humans,
above all.

Srithea Seshen writes about humans—their emotions, psychology, behaviour, contradictions, and the strange ways in which people find, lose, hurt, understand, and love one another.

The Stories

Fiction gives the story its shape. Humanity gives it its truth.

Raw emotion is at the heart of the work.

Not emotion polished into something convenient or beautiful, but emotion as it exists: irrational, inconvenient, tender, ugly, overwhelming, and occasionally transformative.

Feeling deeply is not weakness. It is often where strength begins.

Growth, however, is rarely graceful. There are wrong choices, consequences, losses, and regrets. There are moments when falling is unavoidable.

What matters is the getting up—the decision to continue, to learn, to change, and to try again.

To grow, one must sometimes fall. To live, one must get back up.
