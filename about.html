About

Fiction about people before plots.

About the choices that shape us, the relationships that undo us, and the hope that asks us to begin again.

Srithea
Seshen

Srithea Seshen writes about people: their emotions, their contradictions, their private histories, and the choices that quietly change the course of a life.

The stories move through contemporary romance, literary fiction, family, memory, inheritance, desire, mythology, and the complicated spaces between them.

The work begins with a simple belief: people are more interesting than plots. A plot may tell us what happened. A person tells us why it mattered.

There is room here for tenderness and foolishness, regret and reinvention, obsession and ordinary happiness. Human beings are rarely one thing at a time.

Alongside writing is a life in science. The laboratory offers its own education in patience, observation, uncertainty, failure, and the necessity of beginning again.
