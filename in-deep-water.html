The book

In Deep Water moves beneath the surface of Oakhaven — into the places where desire, fear, loyalty, and memory become difficult to separate.

What looks calm from a distance can contain an entire world beneath it. The same is true of people.

This is a story about the things people conceal, the truths that eventually surface, and what happens when being understood becomes more frightening than being alone.
