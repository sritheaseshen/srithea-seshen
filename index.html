About

Srithea Seshen is a writer of contemporary romance, literary fiction, and human stories.

The work is interested in the spaces between intimacy and memory, mythology and ordinary life, inheritance and choice.

Some stories begin with love. Some begin with what was left behind. Most are interested in what survives both.
