The Writing Desk

Things worth thinking about.

Notes on people, stories, memory, emotion, science, and the strange things that happen when one human being meets another.

Before the plot.

Fiction begins with people.

The plot may decide what happens to them. Character decides what those events mean.

The Writing Desk is where the questions behind the stories get to remain questions for a while: why people stay, why they leave, what they forgive, what they cannot, and how a person becomes someone they did not expect to be.

Some notes will become stories. Some will become nothing at all. That is part of the point.
